# Privacy Policy — Looply

_Last updated: 2026-09-30_

Looply is a personal tool used only by the owner of the Instagram account **@looply_3d** to publish posts and reply to comments on that account.

## What data is accessed
- Posts and comments on @looply_3d, through the official Instagram API.
- Comment text and the commenter's username, used only to write a reply to that comment.

## How it is used
- Data is used only to post content and reply to comments on @looply_3d.
- No data is sold, shared with third parties, or used for advertising.
- No data is collected from anyone outside of comments on @looply_3d posts.

## Storage and retention
- Only the IDs of comments that have been replied to are stored, so the same comment is not answered twice. No other personal data is kept.

## Data deletion
To have any data about you removed, send a direct message to @looply_3d on Instagram, and it will be deleted.

## Contact
Direct message @looply_3d on Instagram.
